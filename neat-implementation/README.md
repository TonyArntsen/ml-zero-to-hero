# NEAT Implementation

NEAT (NeuroEvolution of Augmenting Topologies) evolves both the weights and the structure of neural networks, allowing them to grow over time while preserving useful features.

## Good sources to look up
- The original NEAT paper by Stanley and Miikkulainen
- Stanley's notes and resources on topology evolution
- NEAT Python implementations and tutorials
- Evolutionary computation references on mutation, speciation, and innovation

## Handy math concepts
- Neural network architecture and topology
- Mutation and crossover in structured genomes
- Evolutionary optimization and fitness evaluation
- Probability and stochastic search
- Speciation and diversity maintenance
- Backpropagation is not required for pure NEAT-style training

## Typical things to practice
- Encoding weights and connections as a genome
- Innovation numbers and compatibility distance
- Mutation of weights and network structure
- Maintaining species diversity
- Comparing NEAT to fixed-topology evolution and gradient descent

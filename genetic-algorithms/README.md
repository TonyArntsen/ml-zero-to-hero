# Genetic Algorithms

Genetic algorithms are evolutionary optimization methods inspired by natural selection. They maintain populations of candidate solutions and repeatedly apply selection, crossover, and mutation to improve fitness.

## Good sources to look up
- An Introduction to Genetic Algorithms by Melanie Mitchell
- David E. Goldberg's Genetic Algorithms in Search, Optimization, and Machine Learning
- Eiben and Smith's Introduction to Evolutionary Computing
- Modern evolutionary optimization and DEAP tutorials

## Handy math concepts
- Probability and random sampling
- Fitness landscapes and selection pressure
- Mutation and crossover operators
- Population dynamics and convergence
- Search vs optimization intuition
- No-free-lunch and heuristic trade-offs

## Typical things to practice
- Representing solutions as chromosomes
- Selection strategies like tournament and roulette-wheel
- Mutation and recombination effects
- Tracking convergence over generations
- Comparing GA behavior to hill climbing and local search

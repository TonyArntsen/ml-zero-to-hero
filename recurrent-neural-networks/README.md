# Recurrent Neural Networks

Recurrent neural networks process sequences by maintaining hidden state across time steps, making them useful for time series, text, and other sequential data.

## Good sources to look up
- Deep Learning by Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville
- Christopher Olah's blog on LSTMs and RNNs
- The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks by Karpathy
- Sequence modeling and recurrent network notes from ML courses

## Handy math concepts
- Linear algebra for repeated matrix multiplications
- Calculus through backpropagation through time (BPTT)
- Probability and sequence modeling
- Vanishing and exploding gradients
- State-space intuition and temporal dependencies
- Optimization and regularization for training stability

## Typical things to practice
- Hidden state updates and time-step unrolling
- LSTM and GRU gating mechanisms
- Training on simple text or time-series datasets
- Understanding why long-range dependencies are hard for vanilla RNNs
- Comparing RNNs to transformers on sequence tasks
